# Scientific contract and claim boundaries

1. **Known binding**: assert only when backed by a source with direct experimental measurement and conditions.
2. **Structural evidence**: a deposited ion in a PDB entry supports local geometry under crystallographic conditions, not a measured dissociation constant.
3. **Sequence screening**: acidic-residue fraction and motif hints are features, not affinity predictions.
4. **AlphaFold**: protein confidence or plausible structure is not a binding selectivity result.
5. **Metal selectivity**: evidence must compare at least two ions, with comparable experimental buffer, pH, ionic strength and competition conditions.
6. **No invented citations or geometry**: display missing evidence explicitly.
7. **Human review**: any proposed lab work or separation-performance claim remains a hypothesis until verified.
8. **Synthetic demo**: no synthetic sequence may be represented as a naturally occurring protein or authentic PDB sequence.
9. **Reproducibility**: record database access date, accession, protein sequence checksum, score version, and screening settings.
10. **Agent role**: Argo may propose search strategies and summarize observed results; it must not manufacture biochemical measurements.
